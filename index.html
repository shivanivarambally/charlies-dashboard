Net after rent, six months

Revenue estimate minus rent. Rent includes building charges where the draft lease was read (SITE-001, 006, 008, 009, 010).

  • Sources. BigQuery mcp_retail (historical_sales, market_demand, customer_feedback, candidate_sites) and AlloyDB mcp-retail-cluster (inventory, stock_movements, products, stores). The page embeds small summary tables only, no raw rows.
  • Model. Revenue estimate = forecast six-month ZIP coffee spend x capture rate (20.75% pooled across the six stores, range 16% to 26%). It ignores store size, ramp-up and cannibalization, so it is optimistic for a new store. Net after rent is not profit; no margin data exists.
  • Leases. Rents for SITE-001, 006, 008, 009 and 010 come from the broker's draft leases and include building charges on net leases. The other seven leases were not read, so their rents are base rent only.
  • Construction. The 18-month construction outside SITE-006 and the 25% foot-traffic drop were supplied by the user and are not verified. How much of the drop reaches revenue is unknown, hence the slider.
  • Forecasts vary by about 0.03% to 0.2% between runs of AI.FORECAST. Reviews are synthetic.
